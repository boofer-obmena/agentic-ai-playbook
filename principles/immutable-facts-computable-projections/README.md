# Immutability of Facts and Computability of Projections

> **Принцип неизменяемости фактов и вычисляемости проекций**

Raw data — facts — are the sole source of truth and are never overwritten. Synthesized artifacts — technical specifications, code, reports — are temporary, computed projections. Any correction is applied at the fact layer; derivative artifacts are rebuilt automatically.
